# Practical 2025–2026 research toward quality-preserving target-to-text

Research snapshot: **2026-10-29**, against point2text **`af041dc`**. This is a source review and experiment design; no models were loaded, GPU runs performed, or new success rates measured. Dates below are verified first-public arXiv submission dates, with the version inspected stated separately. Paper results belong to their original tasks and do not demonstrate `1e-4` on this map.

The most useful immediate experiments are **a diverse frontier of completed, canonically scored repairs**, and **a separate sentence/clause paraphrase bank with measured feedback**. Increasing sparse inner updates or the maximum number of combined word edits is a weaker first bet. The current report records no general held-out target reaching `1e-4`; the easy bank-generated witnesses are a different experiment. Its later six-anchor comparison found identical outputs with and without larger combinations, and final text review approved only two. Those observations argue for better proposal coverage, basin retention and quality evidence before increasing combinatorial limits. See [system report](system-report/report.md) and [optimization progress](optimization-progress.md).

The package now includes [budgeted fresh basins and sentence repairs](canonical-restarts.md) and a [separate target-blind sentence bank](sentence-bank.md). [Actual six-click experiments](optimization-progress.md) record their gains, rejected text, and null results. Deliberate diverse-parent terminal lookahead and online residual calibration remain further work.

**What actually needs solving.** Let the ordinary, normalized encoder be `e(x)`, saved PCA rows be `V`, mean be `mu`, and target be `y*`. Success requires an eligible decoded paragraph with

`||V(e(x) - mu) - y*|| <= 1e-4`.

That is an unsquared distance; squared loss must be at most `1e-8`. An improved expected-token loss, rounded coordinates, native confidence, grammar-schema compliance or high full-vector cosine similarity cannot substitute for this test. Keep the existing two fresh singleton encodings in `CanonicalVerifier.confirm_epsilon`, together with a separate text-quality decision. The local judge's documented false passes mean a judge pass is evidence from one screen, not scientific certification.

A 2D target is also **not a full sentence embedding**. It leaves 382 dimensions unspecified under the default encoder. Those dimensions can change claims, subject matter and fluency without much map movement. Conversely, preserving one fixed paragraph's claims can make a particular target inaccessible from its paraphrase neighborhood. Use several task-relevant, independently acceptable starting basins when permitted; do not silently weaken the task or substitute irrelevant but nearby prose.

**Prerequisite: audit executed guidance before interpreting comparisons.** In the inspected baseline `af041dc`, `GuidanceConfig.start_fraction=0.2`, and `_due` starts at `round(start_fraction * (num_steps - 1))`. For 32 requested passes, the first eligible step is **6, zero-based**. `scripts/evaluate_quality_refinement.py::prepare` sets `inner_updates` without overriding that window, whereas the CLI explicitly forwards its configured window. If native stopping ends a trajectory after two to four passes, this harness executes **zero guided steps**, regardless of whether one or sixteen inner updates were requested. The subsequent [fresh-click audit](experiments/fresh-native-paired-summary-v1.json) found that all twelve primary trajectories were actually guided: 142 native passes, 28 guided passes and 448 encoder updates. Do not generalize the conditional zero-update case to historical receipts without inspecting their traces. The preparation harness now exposes its window and defaults to step zero, matching the CLI; older pool settings remain recorded unchanged.

For every new arm, store requested native steps, actual native passes, hook calls, guided-step count, the sum of executed inner updates, applied logit-change norms, start/end fractions, retained top-k probability mass and early-stop reason. A zero-update run is not evidence that sixteen executed updates were ineffective. Make the experimental window explicit, such as starting at step zero when testing early guidance, and report its difference from older harness defaults. Match **executed** native work and encoder calls as well as requested budgets; report any unmatched remainder. Preserve native stopping unless a deliberately separate fixed-step ablation is labeled. This audit is cheaper and more urgent than selecting a new guidance paper.

The older [Vec2Text paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06816) (2023-10-10; [official implementation](https://github.com/vec2text/vec2text)) is useful background for iterative decode → re-embed → correct. It uses trained inversion/correction models for particular embedding spaces. Reusing its feedback architecture is reasonable; passing two PCA coordinates to a pretrained full-vector corrector is not.

**The important inference gap.** `guidance.py` optimizes a frozen top-k support of generator tokens mapped to averaged canonical input vectors. Actual joined text can change token counts, order and attention. More inner updates optimize that approximation more thoroughly; they do not remove its mismatch. Native clean-draft observations already measure real text, so merely “score intermediate drafts” would not be a new contribution.

`refinement.py` already permits some non-improving states in a distance-ranked bounded beam. The missing feature is deliberate basin diversity: the nearest four can all be small variants of one ancestor, and native repair is initiated from `best`, not a selected diverse frontier member. `paraphrases.py` also deliberately restricts `_SURFACE` to **1–8 alphabetic words with limited internal punctuation**. Whole sentences currently fail that conservative guard. Add a separate sentence proposal path; do not remove protections from the existing word-edit path. `combinations.py` measures single-edit displacements but ranks combinations using their additive sum. Exact canonical scoring remains necessary because attention interactions are nonadditive. These are concrete extension seams, not evidence that any proposed extension works.

**Ranked experiments.** All arms should start from the same approved texts, use the same fixed map and have separate ledgers for canonical calls, native forwards, proposal-model calls and judge calls. A proposed pilot budget is **600 canonical text scores per target, including two reserved confirmations**. Match compute between arms rather than giving a new method extra trajectories unnoticed.

1. **Completed-repair frontier — implement today; highest priority.** Keep the best incumbent, plus reserved exploratory states from different approved ancestors and displacement directions. Draw two protected-span repairs from each of two selected parents; score the finished text and any already available clean checkpoints. A round of `2 parents × 2 branches × 8 native passes` costs up to 32 requested native passes, before early stopping; compare actual pass/update counts rather than assuming all 32 execute. Compare against four independent eight-pass repairs from the incumbent. Preserve a fixed claim reference within each basin and a task-relevance screen across basins. A diversity slot may contain a quality-approved state worse in distance than the incumbent, with an explicit exploration cap; reported `best` stays monotone. This is heuristic lookahead beam search inspired by clean-sample/particle methods, not a stationary-distribution or NSMC claim. It needs no training, but executing the trial needs the native GPU slot.

2. **Hierarchical paraphrase coverage — implement the CPU interface today; generate banks later.** Supply a separately versioned JSON bank of reviewed whole-sentence or clause substitutions with exact offsets, then combine replacements at distinct sentences using the existing measured planner. Freeze entities, claim tuples, quantities, negation and qualification relative to the approved reference; review the resulting paragraph, not just each isolated replacement. A controlled pilot can measure 32 atoms, 128 combinations and a second rebased bank while staying under 600 scores. Compare against the current word-only bank at equal calls. Report angular/radial coverage of measured edit displacements and accepted output diversity. More combinations are worthwhile only if the new bank supplies missing directions. A target-blind provider keeps proposal quality separate from coordinate ranking; there is no need to tell a language model what positive PC1 “means.”

3. **Online residual calibration — small CPU experiment, lower confidence.** Record the error between additive predicted coordinates and actual pair/combination coordinates. Fit a bounded, regularized local correction using a small feature set, such as summed displacement, span separation, sentence identities and token-length changes. Reserve measured combinations as a holdout; compare prediction/ranking error against the current additive baseline before using the correction. Choose subsequent probes by both predicted improvement and uncertainty/directional coverage, and rebase after a genuine accepted move. This borrows Zero2Text's online-calibration principle, not its text-inversion algorithm. It may be unhelpful when few interactions are measured; stop rather than fitting a large pair-feature model to tiny data.

4. **Proper SMC or clean-state MH — research implementation after the simpler search comparison.** Start on a small reference model/sampler with explicit probabilities. Audit forward noise, temperature, top-k support, token-acceptance decisions, protected positions, EOS handling, self-conditioning and proposal-path terms. Then consider state cloning and particle batching in the native adapter. With `N` outer particles, `M` proposals and `K` terminal reconstructions, model/reward work can multiply substantially; resampling can also destroy text diversity. A narrow `1e-4` reward window makes degeneracy particularly likely. Anneal the coordinate temperature and measure effective sample size; never imply convergence from a handful of particles. Scientific-quality checks remain external even if a sampling proof is correct.

5. **Formal constraints / relaxed projection — useful support work, not the main epsilon bet.** Try automata for exact formatting, forbidden structural tokens and required literals, or augmented-Lagrangian guidance for a smooth constraint. Keep actual grammar/claim review separate. Exact token-string constraints are tractable in ways that an arbitrary contextual sentence-encoder ball is not; a finite automaton encoding every acceptable paragraph would be impractical. The huge generator vocabulary and tokenizer/byte mapping need an explicit cost audit before borrowing another model's overhead numbers.

6. **A new trained inverse — defer.** If inference-only search plateaus, train a separate proposal/corrector on the exact pinned encoder and task distribution, with residual-conditioned rather than only full-vector-conditioned inputs. That leaves the main encoder/generator frozen but is still an auxiliary training project. Declare data, compute, split and checkpoint changes. Existing inverse checkpoints or short-text recovery scores do not remove this requirement.

A minimal controller experiment can reuse the current verifier:

```text
frontier := quality-approved initial paragraphs with fixed ancestor references
best := nearest eligible frontier paragraph
reserve 2 fresh canonical calls for epsilon confirmation
while budget remains:
    parents := choose distance + ancestor/direction diversity (not nearest only)
    proposals := frozen clause/sentence bank for each parent
    if native budget available:
        proposals += independent protected-span terminal repairs of parents
    measured := ordinary-token canonical scores of mechanically eligible texts
    shortlist := promising states + bounded exploratory/diverse states
    approved := review actual shortlisted paragraphs against fixed references
    retain best eligible incumbent
    frontier := deduplicate and retain approved diverse states within cap
    if stalled: rebase/expand a documented bank or change approved ancestor
    if best.distance <= 1e-4:
        require two fresh singleton canonical encodings, plus final text review
        return success only if every required check passes
return best, budgets, failures and explicit unresolved tolerance
```

**Why full-vector inversion still leaves a research problem.** For the default unit-normalized encoder, set `a = y* + V mu`. Since the rows of `V` are orthonormal, every possible normalized embedding must satisfy `||a|| <= 1`. An outside target therefore has the relaxed lower bound `distance >= max(0, ||a|| - 1)`. This cheap diagnostic can be implemented on cached PCA arrays, allowing for measured normalization/orthogonality error near the boundary; it is not a fluent-text reachability test and does not apply unchanged to unnormalized profiles. A target outside the *sampled corpus hull* is not thereby impossible.

Even an algebraic full-vector lift is non-unique. For a unit null-space vector `n`,

`e_lift = V^T a + sqrt(1 - ||a||^2) n`

has the requested projection when the bound holds. Choose `n` near an approved anchor's null-space direction if experimenting with an inverter. This derivation is our geometric observation, not a claimed paper result. The lift need not be in the image of any real text encoder; normalizing an arbitrary minimum-distance lift can also move its projection. An inverter may decode fluent text whose canonical projection still misses the target. Canonical scoring cannot be bypassed.

**Decision criteria.** First retain target-only held-out witnesses without exposing their text, arbitrary feasible clicks, and local bank-generated fixtures as three separate evaluation groups. Freeze banks/seeds and count missing approved anchors as failures, not silently excluded cases. Report fresh-confirmed epsilon hits, quality-approved distance distributions, independent blinded final-text decisions, topic relevance, quality rejection/uncertainty rates, prediction-versus-actual displacement error and total compute with requested-versus-executed guidance counts. Include repeated seeds and null-effect comparisons. The immediate question is whether improved proposal coverage and diverse completed repairs beat the current search at equal cost while preserving acceptable prose. The reviewed literature makes that experiment plausible; it does not establish that arbitrary quality-preserving `1e-4` targets are reachable.
